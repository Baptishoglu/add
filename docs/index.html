About Me

My name is Berkay Aptishoglu, and I am a Software Technology Engineering student at VIA University College in Horsens, Denmark. I have been living in Denmark for the past five years, having lived in Copenhagen until I moved to Horsens to begin my studies.

Before starting my education in software engineering, I spent 10 years working professionally in the culinary industry, including experience as a Chef de Partie and Sous Chef. Working in professional kitchens taught me valuable skills such as discipline, teamwork, communication, time management, working under pressure, attention to detail, problem-solving, and taking responsibility. I believe these qualities are highly transferable to software development and will help me become a reliable and effective software engineer.

I am now transitioning my career into software engineering and currently developing my skills in Java, Python, HTML, and CSS. I enjoy learning new things, challenging myself, and continuously improving my skills. My goal is to build a long-term career in software engineering and combine the professional discipline and problem-solving mindset I developed through my previous career with my growing technical knowledge.

I consider myself hardworking, eager to learn, and motivated to develop both professionally and personally. I see my career change not as starting from zero, but as bringing 10 years of professional experience and transferable skills into a new field.
